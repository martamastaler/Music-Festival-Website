About us

Primaziza Fest 2026 is a new music festival that will be held in Leicester for the first time exclusively for students of the University of Leicester and De Montfort University. The event will take place from 4 to 6 June 2026 and will bring together artists you definitely wouldn’t expect to see on one stage. This year’s programme will open with the return of Gorillaz with their new album The Mountain, a wildly anticipated performance by Massive Attack, and a special show by the legendary my bloody valentine. The line-up combines five decades of musical styles from indie and shoegaze to pop, hip-hop and electronica. We are known for our inclusivity and this will be a festival that everyone will enjoy.
